# Faction farming route balance

This packet compares one focal RaidBot against seven unchanged RaidBots on authoritative 40 Hz live-content maps. Each pair uses the same map seed, focal seat and faction, one of the ten legacy-authored build goals, and the same 20-minute clock. Only the focal camp chooser or the explicitly named farm rule differs. Snapshots at active 5, 10, 15 and 20 minutes preserve dead players' earned inventory and mark unreached snapshots separately. Travel is the actual focal player's moving ticks; normal camp clears are the server's `camps_cleared` counter. The evaluator scores every earned mob stack by the selected build's declared legacy weight (minimum 0.25), then reports pooled paired value ratio, per-map median, win/tie/loss count, faction and build splits, survival, raw stacks, clears and movement. Mirrored equal-weapon combat checks are limited to pairs alive at the snapshot.

## Frozen source baseline

The pre-tuning `strategy_route_lab` binary is retained at `.agent-runs/alpha-farming-baseline/strategy_route_lab` (SHA-256 `671e285488ecc3dd18b57e271e89b13fa4bd8f9d854c63b59884dc417bfa5fe7`). The earlier 100-map target-vs-nearest route result is in `docs/evidence/raid-routing-ttk/`; at 10 minutes the authored-weight pooled value was 61.2 vs 64.9, ratio 0.942. By faction, target/nearest was Fire 0.893, Sentenced 0.594, Plague 1.233, Frost 0.759, Holy 1.423. Target walked roughly 63 seconds longer and earned roughly 38 fewer raw stacks. In the equal-budget combat check its stat mix scored 89.5%; its minute-earned kit scored 36.3%. These measures distinguish composition quality from time spent getting it.

## Candidate policies and predeclared samples

- `calibrated`: retains the authored target score and eligible camps, changing only its clear-time estimate from the RaidBot 10 seconds per mob to the source planner's 4 seconds. Exploratory seeds 101–110 gave 10-minute pooled value ratio 1.252 and 8/10 paired value wins, but the interval was wide. Held-out seeds 121–170 were predeclared before inspecting them.
- `safe-target`: uses the calibrated target and the two closest eligible fallback camps; the ordinary RaidBot arbiter prices risk among the three. Its exploratory seeds are 171–180. No held-out promotion is allowed from those seeds alone.
- `economic` (diagnostic only): removed after exploratory seeds 111–120 showed 5-minute value ratio 2.19 and 2.2 more clears than nearest, disrupting the intended opening pace. It was not promoted.

The completed calibrated held-out result (50 maps, five per authored build) rejected the exploratory gain. At 10 minutes the pooled ratio was **0.982** (map-bootstrap interval **0.814–1.202**), median paired ratio 1.019, with 25 better, 2 tied and 23 worse maps. It had 37 alive players against nearest's 40, 21.6 fewer raw stacks, 2.98 fewer clears, and 78.2 more moving seconds on average. At 5 minutes the pooled ratio was 0.893 (interval 0.774–1.028), 1.52 fewer clears, and 25.6 more moving seconds: the opening pace was worse. Ten-minute faction ratios were Fire 0.759, Sentenced 0.925, Plague 1.273, Frost 1.491, Holy 0.893. The apparent gain on Frost/Plague did not survive pooling across factions. These are earned inventories including eliminated players, not a conditional-on-survival stat-mix comparison.

The complete safe-target exploration (10 maps, one per authored build) was better at controlling travel: at 10 minutes its pooled ratio was **1.173** (bootstrap interval **0.666–1.879**), median paired ratio 1.218, with 6 better, 1 tied and 3 worse maps. It had 8 alive players against nearest's 9, 5.1 more raw stacks, 0.5 more clears and 37.4 fewer moving seconds on average. At 5 minutes it had 0.1 fewer clears and 18.8 fewer moving seconds. This is promising route-quality evidence, but does not establish the requested 1.25–1.50 band.

The optional `ThemedFocus` product prototype keeps existing themed camp placement and the first-camp reward. After a player clears **any normal camp while near it**, qualifying normal kills at a *different* normal camp paying that faction's declared focus stat earn three additional stacks. The origin camp may pay another stat; a same-site respawn never pays the bonus, and an elite camp, remote clear or training camp cannot arm it. The same server rule applies to human and bot seats, and nearest gets the exact same bonus when it follows an eligible route. It remains off in `live_content()` while measured. Focus stats: Fire Damage; Sentenced AttackSpeed; Plague AP; Frost AttackSpeed; Holy AttackSpeed. Existing camp stat icons identify the reward stat, and the server kill toast reports the actual grant. A Help Guide explanation and base-versus-conditional badge copy are required before any live switch.

The first product comparison used the stricter requirement that the origin itself pay the focus stat, with unchanged authored `TargetBuild` versus nearest under that common rule. Its exploratory seeds were 181–190; partial 191–230 and 241–250 files were stopped after this prerequisite proved too sparse in real maps and were not used for promotion. JSONL `focus_bonus_kills` counts actual paid +3 kill toasts. From total earned weighted value `V` and weighted qualifying kill count `E`, the evaluator derives the pre-bonus value `B = V - 3E`. For a route-to-nearest ratio of 1.375, the fitted additional stacks per qualifying kill solve `(Bt + p Et)/(Bn + p En) = 1.375`; this is diagnostic only, not a substitute for prospective replay.

Across those ten strict-rule exploratory maps, target earned 19 paid bonuses versus nearest's 0 by minute 10, yet its pooled weighted-value ratio was only **1.177** with 6 better and 4 worse maps, and it moved 50.1 seconds longer on average. At minute 5 its ratio was 1.312, with only four paid bonuses. The route's travel cost overwhelmed most of the conditional reward.

The revised product rule permits any normal origin clear, preserving the first camp's payout and making the route usable. A LAB-only `focus-route` chooser was fixed before independent exploratory seeds 251–260 and predeclared held-out seeds 261–310. It retains two local risk-priced fallbacks; the first focus camp may be at most four estimated walking seconds beyond the closest eligible camp, and a different follow-on focus camp at most ten seconds beyond it. Candidate value reflects the real 6-versus-3 paid stacks after a tracked normal clear, while the ordinary RaidBot risk arbiter still prices death and travel. No live bot policy uses this chooser.

The forced `nearest` arm allows only one camp into the arbiter, so a higher route value can come from safer camp choice rather than stat targeting. The follow-up controls keep the **identical** two-local-plus-focus candidate shortlist and ordinary risk arbiter. `focus-risk` runs the same `ThemedFocus` payout without a focus candidate-value premium; `theme-risk` and `theme-stat` both use ordinary `Themed` payout, with the latter multiplying each camp value by the exact declared weight of its visible reward stat for that seed's authored build. `focus-live` is the untouched shipping RaidBot focal policy under the same `ThemedFocus` payout. These controls separate paid affinity, authored stat preference and combat risk. A live rule remains off until the equal-risk results, faction splits and survival support it.

## Reproduction

`cargo run --release -p rh-headless --features strategy-route-lab --bin strategy_route_lab -- 10 171 safe-target > output.jsonl 2> results.jsonl`

`python3 tools/balance-lab/route_tuning_eval.py TRIAL.jsonl NEAREST.jsonl evaluation.json --duel .agent-runs/alpha-farming-baseline/camp_bench`

The `--duel` option is omitted for a fast inventory-only evaluation. The source code and per-map JSONL are retained under `.agent-runs/alpha-farming-baseline/`; that directory is an untracked runtime artifact.
