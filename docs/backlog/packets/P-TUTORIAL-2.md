## 1. ID and outcome
The engine-free library knows the tutorial: (a) `AccountFullProfile` carries optional `Tutorial` (`AccountTutorialState`
version/step/state) and `TutorialDue` (missing → null/false, so older servers parse); (b) `AccountApiService.SaveTutorialAsync(version, step, state, ct)`
posts to `/api/profile/{name}/tutorial` exactly like the equip mutation (owner, gate, `x-profile-token`, transport),
publishes the returned profile on 200, never throws, and leaves nothing pending; (c) a pure `TutorialFlow` state machine
and a read-only `TutorialObserver` under `client-unity/RoguehallaNet/Net/Ui/`. Steps: 0 Welcome (explicit Start only) →
1 Move into a ring (own position within 48 u of a point 120 u from the first observed position toward the nearest rack)
→ 2 Take a weapon with F (own weapon or inventory slot no longer Fists) → 3 Hit the plain training dummy (Fx Hit by own
id on an `ActorKind.TrainingDummy` actor not holding a Shield) → 4 Swap with Q/1/2 (both slots armed, then
`ActiveSlot` changes) → 5 Cast your Wish (Wish goes from idle to busy: cast record, charges spent or `ReadyAt` in the
future; already-recharging on entry does not count) → 6 Done. Each step exposes copy and a target (none / point+radius /
actor id / container id, with X/Y). Wish copy: "Press {wish} to cast your Wish (click to place it if it aims)".
`Skip`, `Restart`, `ResumeAt(step)` (clamped).
