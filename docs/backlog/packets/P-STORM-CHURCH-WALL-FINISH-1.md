## Acceptance checks

All eight placements must put the wall finish on each church wall cell. Each blocked base must read at normal play size. Distinguish the continuous coping and blind tracery from individual stones. Walk both doors and pew banks in the browser. Recheck occlusion, brightness and cost beside the Fire/Bailiff encounter.

## Validation commands

## Risk and suggested model tier

Visual scale and edge continuity at the gameplay camera. Unity presentation implementer.

## Known traps

A broad cap can resemble a single enormous brick. Use small stone courses and a continuous rim on engaged and standalone walls. Read the actual wall mask before changing the UVs.
