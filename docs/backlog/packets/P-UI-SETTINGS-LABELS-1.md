## 1. ID and outcome

When a player opens the settings card (gear) or the Help window in a match, every label and note stands at its real size from the first opening, not at 6 px, and both windows show where they scroll.

1. **The card refits after it has laid itself out.** `Fit(width)` runs in this order **[choice]**: (a) `_root.sizeDelta = (width, current height)`; (b) `Canvas.ForceUpdateCanvases()` and `LayoutRebuilder.ForceRebuildLayoutImmediate(_content)` (rows take the new width before the height is read); (c) the final `_root.sizeDelta = (width, min(36 + preferred content height + 10, _maxHeight))`; (d) only when `_root.gameObject.activeInHierarchy`, `RefitLabels()`: `Refit()` on every `MythicTextScale` under `_root` with `FitBox` true, collected with `_root.GetComponentsInChildren(false, _fits)` (active only: an inactive label refits by `OnEnable` when it wakes) into one `readonly List<MythicTextScale> _fits` (no array per call); (e) in the `finally`, a private `float _fitScale = _source.TextScale`. Hidden, nothing is laid out and nothing refits. A label at its wanted size writes nothing; a shrunk label is dirtied by the measuring write in `MythicTextScale.Fits` (`MythicTextScale.cs:77-85`). `Fit` runs: at the opening; on a viewport change (`MatchHud.Features.cs:99` subscribes `ApplyViewport` to both stage scalers, each reaches `_modernSettings.ApplyViewport` through `ApplyLegacyViewport`, `MatchHud.Legacy.cs:358`: twice per changed frame); on a display-preference click while open (`MatchHud.ApplyDisplayPreferences`, `MatchHud.Features.cs:150`, runs `ApplyLegacyViewport(_viewport)`); and on a text-size change while open (item 2). `Fit` sets `_root.sizeDelta` after its layout today (`ModernSettingsView.cs:115`): on a resize a refit placed right after that layout would still see the old width, hence step (a).
2. **The open card follows a text-size change, and only that.** `ModernSettingsView` subscribes a new `void SettingsChanged()` to `_source.Changed` in place of `Refresh` (`:96`; `:146` in `OnDestroy`); `_prefs.Changed` stays `Refresh`. `SettingsChanged()` is `Refresh();` then `Fit(_root.sizeDelta.x)` when `!_fitting`, `_root.gameObject.activeInHierarchy` and `!Mathf.Approximately(_source.TextScale, _fitScale)`: only a text-size change alters a row's width or height (`MatchHud` subscribed `ApplyTextScale` first, `MatchHud.cs:75`, body `:122-136`, so the labels hold the new scale by then). A volume tick, a quality change, reduced motion and an adaptive step fit nothing (the volume setters raise Changed on every tick, `ShowcaseSettings.cs:164-166`). A private `bool _fitting`, true for the whole of `Fit` (try/finally), stops a Changed raised inside `Fit` from calling it again: the Editor's slider echo (see "Oracle") comes out of `Fit`'s own `ForceUpdateCanvases` when a card is built and opened with no canvas update between, as in `ModernSettingsLayoutEditModeTests` (read, not run: `Slider.cs:410`). Cost: one canvas update, one content rebuild and 40 label refits per text-size change while the card is open; a drag of the Text size slider fits once per tick, which it needs, and moves the slider row down by up to about 12 units (the drag reads the pointer's x only).

The smallest mechanism for the label line **[choice]**: the label (alignment `MidlineLeft`, as in the other rows) gets a `LayoutElement` with `minHeight` 20 (the switch's height), so the label line is `H = max(text height, 20)` high and the 36 x 20 switch fits in it, and a TMP `margin` right of `SwitchReserve` = 48 (switch 36 + 12), so its text leaves the switch's place while its box spans the row. ## 11. Known traps
- The experiment ran only the refit (its remedy). The stacked row and `Unfit` were read from the code; the oracle author's throwaway implementation made every test green before this oracle was committed (see below).
- `Unfit` goes where the label is made: `HelpGuideView` sets the text afterwards, and a still-fitted label would refit in `OnEnable` against the 0-high box. `Unfit` never enables auto-sizing.
- EditMode delivers no `OnEnable` or `LateUpdate` to `MythicTextScale` or the `ScrollRect`: tests 9 and 15 call them or the event, as play does. A button press runs no layout: call `Canvas.ForceUpdateCanvases()`. `SetEffective` and `SetTextScale` write the static `Quality`: restore it.

- The packet branch stays linear; the implementer's branch merges `packet/<ID>`, never the reverse.
